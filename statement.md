# Problem Statement
Students often lose track of attendance and discover too late that they are below the 75% requirement.

## Scope
A command-line tool for 4 subjects: enter attendance, calculate percentages, and plan future classes.

## Target Users
College students (e.g., VIT) who want a quick, offline attendance check.

## High-Level Features
- Attendance entry with validation
- Per-subject and overall percentage
- Planner: classes needed / classes you can skip
- Data saved in JSON, activity logged to a file
